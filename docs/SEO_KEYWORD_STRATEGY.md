# Solmere Journal — SEO Keyword Strategy & Content Bets

Reference for building new editorial pages and blog posts. How Google ranks pages and which content bets to place next.

## The Keyword Leverage Hierarchy

Ordered from highest to lowest impact on ranking.

### 1. H1 / Title Tag (Strongest Signal)

The keyword must be in the H1. Exact match or very close natural order.

> Example: If your found keyword is "merino wool t-shirt no shrink", your H1 must be:
> **"Merino Wool T-Shirt No Shrink: Our Top Pick"**

This is the single heaviest on-page signal for Google. If it's not here, you're dead in the water.

### 2. URL Slug

Edit the page URL to include the core keyword.

> Good: `solmere.org/merino-wool-t-shirt-no-shrink`
> Bad: `solmere.org/p12345`

Google reads the URL path as a contextual clue.

### 3. First 100 Words (Introduction)

Mention the keyword naturally in the first 2-3 sentences.

> Example: "If you're tired of replacing shrunken tees, finding a merino wool t-shirt
> with no shrink feels impossible. We tested 5 to find the one that survives the dryer."

Google's crawlers assign higher weight to content near the top of the page.

### 4. At Least One H2 Subheading

Put the keyword (or a close variant) in an H2 further down.

> Example: `<h2>Why a Merino Wool T-Shirt With No Shrink Costs More</h2>`

### 5. Image Alt Text

Add the keyword to the alt text of your product image.

> Example: `alt="Solmere's top pick for a merino wool t-shirt with no shrink"`

Gets you into Google Images (extra traffic) and reinforces the page's topic.

### 6. Internal Link Anchor Text (Indexing Hack)

This is the most overlooked lever. Google finds and indexes new pages primarily through links from other pages on your site.

**How:** Go to your highest-traffic, oldest page (your "breadwinner"). Add a single sentence linking to the new page, using your exact keyword as the clickable text.

> Example: "We also recently tested a **merino wool t-shirt with no shrink** that beat out cotton."

This tells Google: "My most trusted page is vouching for this new page on this specific keyword." Google will crawl and index it within hours instead of days.

## Content Bets (Future Editorial Ideas)

These are thesis-driven content plays targeting underserved keyword spaces.

### Bet 1: The "New Material" Play

**Thesis:** Merino wool blends are replacing cotton in premium everyday wear, but searchers are confused about shrinkage and pilling.

**Seed Page:** "Merino Wool vs Cotton T-Shirt: Which Actually Lasts Longer?" (Comparison page)

**Supporting pages (if it scales):**
- "How to wash merino wool t-shirts"
- "Best merino blend for hot weather"

**Why it wins:** Keyword tools haven't caught the shift from cotton to wool yet. Reddit and niche forums are buzzing about it. You arrive before the big blogs publish their roundups.

---

### Bet 2: The "Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) Alternative" Play

**Thesis:** Incumbent brands (like Everlane or Vionic) are raising prices. People are searching for the "cheaper alternative" but the big SEO sites only review the expensive one.

**Seed Page:** "[Expensive Brand] vs [New DTC Brand]: Is the price tag justified?"

**Supporting pages:**
- "Why [New DTC Brand] uses a different leather"
- "Sizing guide for [New DTC Brand]"

**Why it wins:** The "vs" keyword has massive commercial intent. The big sites don't cover the new DTC brand yet because it's not in their affiliate networks. You jump on it first.

---

### Bet 3: The "Silent Problem" Play

**Thesis:** A specific, annoying problem (e.g., "belt buckle squeaks when walking" or "sneaker tongue slides sideways") has thousands of frustrated forum posts but zero dedicated product pages solving it.

**Seed Page:** "The Only Belt That Doesn't Squeak (We Tested 5)" — directly naming the problem in the H1.

**Supporting pages:**
- "How to stop a belt squeak" (informational, links to the seed)

**Why it wins:** This is bottom-of-funnel. If someone searches for a specific annoyance, they are done researching. They want the fix now. Your page is the only one offering the solution, so they click your Amazon link instantly.
